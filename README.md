# Online Chat Privacy Checklist

A practical reference for people who participate in random video, voice, or text conversations.

This checklist focuses on general privacy habits when communicating with unfamiliar people online.

## Before Starting a Conversation

- Check whether your camera reveals personal documents, photographs, or identifying details.
- Verify which microphone and camera your browser will use.
- Avoid displaying financial information or sensitive notifications.
- Review a platform's privacy policy and community guidelines.
- Decide which personal details you are comfortable sharing.

## During Video Conversations

Video can reveal more than people realize.

Background objects, visible addresses, identification cards, and open browser tabs may expose information unintentionally.

Choose a suitable background and remember that another participant may be able to record or capture your screen.

## During Voice Conversations

Voice chat allows participants to communicate without appearing on camera.

However, background conversations and environmental sounds may still disclose information.

Use a microphone mute control when appropriate and avoid discussing sensitive details in public spaces.

## During Text Conversations

Be cautious with unexpected links, suspicious downloads, and requests for passwords or financial details.

Do not assume that a stranger is trustworthy simply because the conversation feels friendly.

## Conversation Boundaries

You can end an interaction whenever you feel uncomfortable.

Respect the other participant's communication preferences and avoid pressuring people to share information.

## Evaluation Checklist for Chat Platforms

When evaluating an online chat service, consider:

- Are the available communication formats explained clearly?
- Is it easy to leave a conversation?
- Are privacy expectations understandable?
- Can participants find reporting or help information?
- Are account requirements clearly communicated?

## Conclusion

Meeting unfamiliar people online can be interesting and enjoyable, but privacy awareness remains essential.

Simple precautions, clear personal boundaries, and respectful communication contribute to better online experiences.
